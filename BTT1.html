Meet Nightily Furrying

Nightily Furrying is a Mythical Beast that strongly resembles your typical Samoyed dog in shape and size, but with a few key differences.

You've probably seen him before somewhere else with another mythical creature that is his companion, maybe during one of your C Programming contest.

What Does He Look Likes?

Nightily has black fluffy fur and a long bushy tail with a patch of light blue fur on the tip that glows in the dark. A lot different from the usual white that normal Samoyeds have.

He also can be seen always wearing a golden four-pointed star necklace around his neck, which is actually an old gift from his best friend.

What Does He Likess?

He likes a lot of things but you'll mainly see him do stuff like:

  • Programming (His current passion)
  • Making music (Badlly)
  • Drawing (Also Badly)
  • Gardening (Probably badly too)

Some Quick Facts

Here are some interesting facts about Nightily:

a. Can manipulate a lot of things (Doesn't know that yet).
b. He tends to like flashy and complex stuff but has no skill to make them
c. Get tired kinda easily.
d. Had a lot of pets, they're no longer around.
